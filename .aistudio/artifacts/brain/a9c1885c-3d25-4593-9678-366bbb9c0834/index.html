How To Play Mojo Rush

"Your coffee break just got competitive." Master the Mojo drive-thru in 30 seconds!

1
Read the Order Ticket
Each car orders a signature drink. Note the sequence of required ingredients.
2
Tap Ingredients in Order
Tap ingredients (or press 1–9, 0, B, S, C, W) from top to bottom. Watch the cup fill!
3
Trigger Mojo Moments
Complete 3 consecutive orders without mistakes to trigger a MOJO MOMENT for +30 bonus points!
Scoring: Correct step +10 · Completed drink +50 · Perfect pour +25 · Mojo Moment +30
⚠️ Warning: Incorrect ingredients deduct seconds off the clock!
